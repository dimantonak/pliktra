    Song editor

    How to write notes
    • One step per word: C4 D4 E4. The number is the octave; C4 is middle C.
    • Sharps and flats: F#4, Bb3.
    • Keys played together (chord): join with +, e.g. C4+E4+G4.
    • Hands: notes below middle C go to the left hand, the rest to the right. Force a hand with L: or R:, e.g. L:C4+R:E4.
    • Fingers: add / and the finger after a note: E4/3, G3/4+B3/2. 1 = thumb, 2 = index, 3 = middle, 4 = ring, 5 = little finger (same numbers for both hands).
    • Length in beats: C4_2 = 2 beats, G4_0.5 = half a beat (1 beat = a quarter note). - is a rest, -_2 a 2-beat rest.
    • Time signature: write it first, e.g. 3/4 or 6/8 (default 4/4). It sets the bars in the sheet music.
    • Bar lines | and anything after // are ignored.
    • Record from piano adds what you play on the piano to the end of the text.
