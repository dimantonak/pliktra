    Song editor

    How to write notes
    • One step per word: C4 D4 E4. The number is the octave; C4 is middle C.
    • Sharps and flats: F#4, Bb3.
    • Keys played together (chord): join with +, e.g. C4+E4+G4.
    • Hands: notes below middle C go to the left hand, the rest to the right. Force a hand with L: or R:, e.g. L:C4+R:E4.
    • Fingers: add / and the finger after a note: E4/3, G3/4+B3/2. 1 = thumb, 2 = index, 3 = middle, 4 = ring, 5 = little finger (same numbers for both hands).
    • Length in beats: C4_2 = 2 beats, G4_0.5 = half a beat (1 beat = a quarter note). - is a rest, -_2 a 2-beat rest.
    • Time signature: write it first, e.g. 3/4 or 6/8 (default 4/4). It sets the bars in the sheet music.
    • Bar lines | and anything after // are ignored.
    • Record from piano adds what you play on the piano to the end of the text.

    Without a piano

    You can also play with a computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K = C to C, Z / X = octave down / up.

    What the buttons do
    • Both / Right / Left – practise both hands or one hand. The other hand’s notes are then not counted as mistakes.
    • ↺ Restart – start again from the beginning (or from the start of the loop).
    • ✋ Fingers – show or hide finger numbers (1 = thumb … 5 = little finger).
    • ♫ Sheet – sheet music (lit) or falling notes. Imported MIDI files always use falling notes.
    • ▶︎ Listen – hear the song from where you are; tap ■ to stop and play it yourself.
    • ⟳ Loop – repeat a hard part: tap at its first note, move the slider (or play on) to its last note, tap Set end. Tap Loop again to stop.
    • Slider – start from any point in the song.
    • ☰ Songs – choose, add, edit, share and back up songs.
